# Ravenfall — art prompt (source of the reference image)

The prompt used to generate `ravenfall_golden_town.png`. It is the source of truth for
the city spec in `docs/game_design.md` → "Ravenfall city spec".

> Create a high-quality isometric 2.5D fantasy RPG main city map for a PC game called THE FRONTIER. The city is named Ravenfall, a peaceful medieval European frontier town surrounded by ancient stone walls, rolling green hills, and dense forests. The entire city must be visible in one cohesive environment, viewed from a fixed 45-degree isometric angle with a slightly elevated camera.
>
> The town features a large central stone plaza with a beautiful ancient fountain, a grand medieval town hall, a blacksmith with a glowing forge, a cozy tavern with wooden balconies, a general goods shop, a potion shop, a small marketplace with colorful stalls, a church with a tall bell tower, residential cottages with timber frames, flower gardens, stone pathways, wooden fences, and warm lanterns. Include a main entrance gate connected to a winding road leading into the wilderness, clearly establishing the city as the starting point of a grand adventure.
>
> Visual style: premium stylized 3D game environment, charming fantasy realism, handcrafted miniature diorama aesthetic, polished modern indie RPG art direction, soft ambient occlusion, rich environmental details, beautiful natural lighting, warm earthy colors, lush greenery, highly readable building silhouettes, subtle magical atmosphere, detailed stone and wood materials.
>
> Map design: clear spatial organization, interconnected streets, distinct functional districts, accessible open spaces for gameplay, recognizable landmarks, balanced building density, clean navigation routes, seamless cohesive terrain, designed as a practical playable RPG hub rather than a concept painting.
>
> Composition: complete city map fully contained within the image, isometric orthographic projection, consistent scale across all buildings, no cut-off structures, no extreme close-ups, no perspective distortion, no characters blocking the environment.
>
> Ultra-detailed, production-quality game environment, crisp textures, sharp edges, professional game art, 4K quality. No text, no labels, no UI, no HUD, no watermark, no borders.
